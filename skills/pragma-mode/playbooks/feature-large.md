### Feature, multi-session

**You own the plan.** This playbook produces a spec and tickets. Building
happens one ticket at a time in later sessions.

1. Sharpen the idea with [grilling](../../grilling/SKILL.md), recording agreed
   terms and decisions with [domain-modeling](../../domain-modeling/SKILL.md).
2. Settle each question that needs a runnable answer with
   [prototype](../../prototype/SKILL.md). Bridge the prototype's separate
   session with [handoff](../../handoff/SKILL.md) in both directions.
3. Turn the thread into a spec with [to-spec](../../to-spec/SKILL.md).
4. Split the spec with [to-tickets](../../to-tickets/SKILL.md). Each ticket
   names the checks it must pass: its tests, the running-system journeys from
   the project's verification config, and a performance check where relevant.
5. Name the first ready ticket. Each ticket is built in its own session with
   `/pragma-mode` and the [Feature](feature.md) playbook, where the ticket is
   the checkable spec.

When the destination itself is unclear, stop here and use the
[wayfinder](../../wayfinder/SKILL.md) skill instead.

**Reply:** where the spec lives, the tickets with their blocking edges, and
the first ready ticket.
