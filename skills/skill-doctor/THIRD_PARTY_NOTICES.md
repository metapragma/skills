This fork retains the local session parsers and Warp decoder. It adds source
record addressing and version checks, replaces numeric scoring with qualitative
assessment, removes skill-use-weighted sampling and finding quotas, and produces
a self-contained report without external assets or promotional content. Broad
collection remains available and is the default. Skill edits are proposals
unless the user requests application.
