# Third-Party Notices

The primary Matt Pocock source is tracked in `skill-sources.json`. This skill
also borrows from pstack's [prototype playbook](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/e43c7ee26e0038c6c1fa8380dd34ce86ff94cb2a/pstack/skills/poteto-mode/playbooks/prototype.md)
at commit `e43c7ee26e0038c6c1fa8380dd34ce86ff94cb2a`.

The experimental branch selectively adapts the distinction between interactive
prototypes and small behavioral or timing experiments. It reuses real project
code, permits focused assertions, qualifies measurements, and keeps production
changes, capture commits, and publication separately authorized.

The timing guidance also selectively adapts
[Explain the Number](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/e43c7ee26e0038c6c1fa8380dd34ce86ff94cb2a/pstack/skills/principle-explain-the-number/SKILL.md)
at the same commit. It checks completed work, equivalent conditions, and
variability, and separates observed differences from causal explanations and
end-to-end claims. It does not require a fixed sample count or a separate
benchmark skill for a small experiment.
