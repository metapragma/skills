# Third-Party Notices

The primary Matt Pocock source is tracked in `skill-sources.json`. The performance
measurement guidance selectively adapts pstack's
[Explain the Number](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/e43c7ee26e0038c6c1fa8380dd34ce86ff94cb2a/pstack/skills/principle-explain-the-number/SKILL.md)
at commit `e43c7ee26e0038c6c1fa8380dd34ce86ff94cb2a`.

It checks completed work, equivalent conditions, variability, and the scope of
performance claims. Causal explanations require supporting measurements, while
an unknown cause does not invalidate a qualified observation. It does not require
a companion skill, a fixed sample count, or unrelated project changes.
